## Daily AIOS coordinator wiring

- Home and AIOS both expose the same bounded `workspace.daily.coordinate` server action for owner, admin, and operations roles.
- One durable `daily_coordinator` run records a parent gate plus only `crm.deal.route`, `crm.lead.triage`, `inbox.sla.triage`, and `trip.operations.monitor` child evidence.
- The sweep is capped at ten unassigned opportunities, twenty-five lead risks, twenty-five Inbox SLA risks, and one deterministic Radar reconciliation; no customer text enters the parent payload or result.
- Every child re-enters its own policy gate. Parent Auto cannot override child Observe, Assist, disabled, or Approval modes.
- Parent Approval creates no child effect; approval resolution resumes the exact stored run. A child failure is isolated, produces bounded metadata, and marks the aggregate partial without rolling back already-safe idempotent work.
- Authenticated Chromium proves direct execution, exact ledger scope, immediate approval-queue refresh, parent approval/resumption, policy restoration, and the embedded Home control. The pure contract tests prove the external-action boundary and summary behavior.

## Human approval escalation wiring

- A pending approval becomes manually escalatable only after its configured deadline; premature attempts fail closed in PostgreSQL.
- The database serializes the request, derives eligible approvers from the exact action policy, excludes Operations from finance-sensitive decisions, and reroutes to another active human when possible.
- If no alternative exists, the request is assigned or the current human is reminded. Every outcome keeps `status = pending`, renews the deadline, and never executes the gated action.
- `approval_escalation_events` is append-only for the lifetime of its workspace, tenant-readable, browser-non-writable, and paired with bounded `approval.escalated` audit metadata.
- Owners/admins receive a due-only **Escalate now** control. A bearer-protected worker endpoint sweeps at most 25 overdue gates for deployment scheduling.
- Escalation atomically creates or updates one open internal task assigned to the selected human. The task is due immediately, links to `/aios/approvals`, and completes only when the approval leaves Pending.
- Hosted Chromium proves overdue visibility, rerouting to the next eligible human, persisted evidence, renewed timing, Tasks-to-approval navigation, task completion, and a subsequent explicit human approval.
